Welcome to the World of AI!

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines designed to think like humans and mimic their actions.

What is AI?

AI involves creating algorithms that can learn, adapt, and perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation.
